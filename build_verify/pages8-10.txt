      5   C ONCLUSION
487
488
      We presented OSTRA, a framework for multi-stage manipulation that represents historical and fu-
489
      ture object states in a shared Object-State Transition Map. Transition-Guided Attention and Staged
490   Action Generation progressively translate object-state changes into TCP subgoals and executable
491   actions. Across RLBench2, ManiSkill, LIBERO, and seven real-world tasks, OSTRA consistently
492   outperforms representative imitation-learning baselines, while the ablations validate the contribu-
493   tions of OST-Map and the proposed architectural designs. Future work will improve map con-
494   struction under perception errors and extend object-state reasoning to less structured, open-world
495   manipulation.
